# Bee’s Auto Repair

Build a polished, responsive website for a local US automotive repair and vehicle maintenance business website called “BEE'S Auto Repair” 

GOAL
Help local customers understand the shop's services, trust the business, call the shop, and request an appointment.

DESIGN DIRECTION
- Modern, professional automotive service brand.
- Deep navy, white , and restrained blue accents
- Strong typography, generous spacing, subtle transitions, and high-quality automotive workshop imagery.
- Premium and trustworthy, not flashy or cluttered.
- Design mobile-first and make the desktop layout look equally professional.

HOMEPAGE SECTIONS
1. Header with logo, Services, location, About, Reviews, Contact, and a prominent Call Now button.
2. Hero with the headline “Reliable Auto Repair. Honest Service. Back on the Road.”, supporting copy, a Request an Appointment button, and a call-to-action.
3. Service cards for vehicle diagnostics, brake repair, oil changes, engine repair, AC service, and routine maintenance.
4. Why Choose Us section.
5. How It Works section explaining diagnosis, estimate approval, repair, and vehicle collection.
6. Customer reviews section with clearly marked sample placeholders, never presented as genuine reviews.
7. Appointment request form design.
8. Contact section with placeholders for address, phone, hours, and service area.
9. Footer with navigation and privacy-policy link.

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
- Responsive navigation and mobile-friendly layouts.
- Phone links must use a placeholder phone number until the real number is provided.
- Buttons must have clear hover, focus, and active states.
- Appointment fields should include name, contact details, vehicle year/make/model, requested service, and preferred date.
- Make it clear that an appointment is not confirmed until the business responds.
- Do not claim certifications, warranties, customer ratings, prices, or business details that have not been verified.
- Do not invent a US address or map location.
- Do not add payments, authentication, a database, or paid integrations yet.

TECHNICAL APPROACH
Use a maintainable, accessible frontend. Explain the framework and file structure you choose. Keep components reusable and avoid unnecessary dependencies.

First create the homepage visual design and responsive layout. Show me what has been created before moving on to the other sections. Explain the key components and the changes you make so I can learn from the code.
